Data and method

Disasters on social media, originally from CrowdFlower / Figure Eight, is listed as CC0 by its distributor. Disaster relevance is a supplied human annotation; sentiment is independently estimated using Cardiff NLP’s Twitter RoBERTa sentiment model.

The sample is drawn after cleaning, with random seed 401. Source tweet IDs are rounded, so records use the source’s unique annotation ID. The data has no usable tweet timestamp or engagement count. Original text is preserved; remaining source encoding artifacts may affect predictions.
