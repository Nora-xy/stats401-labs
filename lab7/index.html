OBSERVATIONS FROM THE DATA

How the network evolves

Compare three equal 20-day periods. Connectivity means daily relationships and connected components; activity means incident dollar volume, rather than a formal centrality score.

PeriodMean links / dayMean components / day¹Mean value / dayCross-region link share²

¹ Includes isolated companies. ² Cross-regional relationship-days divided by all relationship-days; each active relationship on each day counts once.

  1. Connectivity increases late in the series

    Mean daily links rise from 5.50 in days 1–20 to 5.60 in days 21–40 and 7.25 in days 41–60. Mean daily components fall from 6.50 to 5.05 between the first and last periods. The later network is more connected on average, though individual days still fluctuate.

  2. The most active companies change

    Fusion Electronics grows from $372,117 in incident volume in days 1–20 to $1,053,610 in days 21–40. In the final period, Ion Systems ($1,096,550) and Cedar Retail ($901,810) lead, ahead of Fusion ($559,804). Larger circles reveal this shift in activity; dollar volume alone does not establish structural centrality.

  3. Separate components persist

    Day 1 has three disconnected pairs and six isolated companies: nine components in total. Daily components average 6.50 in each of the first two periods and 5.05 in the last. Fragmentation persists even as connectivity grows. Spatial proximity alone does not indicate a connected cluster; follow the visible links.

  4. New relationships reshape the later network

    Fusion Electronics–Ion Systems is absent in days 1–40, then active on 10 of the final 20 days, totaling $300,580. BlueRiver Logistics–Harbor Shipping also appears only in the final period, on seven days ($121,461). Apex Textiles–Fusion Electronics persists across all three periods, with 7, 6, and 7 active days respectively. Lines can disappear between daily snapshots without implying a permanent business separation.

  5. The network becomes more cross-regional

    The cross-regional share of daily relationships is 63.6%, 63.4%, and 72.4% across the three periods. The final period therefore has more cross-regional connectivity by this measure. Link details show the two regions; this share counts relationships rather than dollars.

Data & method. Fabricated course datasets, not real commercial activity. Companies CSV · Transactions CSV. Dates are parsed with D3. A force simulation on the union of all relationships establishes a fixed layout; keyed joins update daily edges and node volumes. Node volume counts each relationship at both endpoints; the daily network total counts each record once.
