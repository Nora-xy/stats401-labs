Lab 2: Multivariate Visualization with D3

Explore how population, average temperature, development level, and region vary across twelve cities.

Design Justification

I mapped population to horizontal position because it is a ratio variable with a meaningful zero, making city-size comparisons direct. Average temperature is shown vertically so viewers can compare it against population. Region uses color because it is a nominal category with no natural ordering. Development level uses circle size: larger circles indicate higher levels from Low to Medium to High. Hovering over a point reveals the city name and exact values, which helps viewers notice regional patterns and see whether larger or more developed cities tend to have different temperatures.
